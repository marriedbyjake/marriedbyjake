It was an absolute honour having Jake as our celebrant. We couldn't have asked for a better celebrant for our wedding. Daren and I wanted a pastor who could infuse our Christian ceremony with faith and joy, and Jake delivered the perfect balance of both! It was everything we hoped for and more.

He went above and beyond, consistently checking in on us at the altar and making sure we were comfortable and at ease during the entire ceremony. His ability to captivate the audience with every word was truly remarkable, and what made it even more special was how he seamlessly incorporated our personal stories into his presentation, making the ceremony feel deeply personal and unique.

Our guests were equally impressed - so much so that many of them asked where we found him! They were astonished to learn that we handpicked him and that he travelled all the way from Queensland to be part of our special day.

We also wanted to thank him for his incredible support in announcing the order of photos for the photographers, especially knowing he had a flight to catch and it was raining. His dedication made a huge difference and we truly appreciate his effort to ensure everything ran smoothly.

We highly recommend him to anyone looking for a thoughtful, engaging and professional celebrant!
