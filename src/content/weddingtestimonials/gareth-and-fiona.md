As soon as we met Jake for the first time, we knew we wanted him to be the one who married us. Throughout the planning process he was so helpful and such a pleasure to deal with. Nothing was too much trouble.

Any questions we had, he was more than happy to answer, and we really appreciated the guidance he gave us when it came to the structure of the ceremony and writing our own vows.

On the day, he immediately made us feel at ease and took away any nerves with his positive and reassuring approach. He really took the time to make the ceremony ours and so many of our guests noticed this too.

Jake's really amazing at what he does and we would highly recommend him to anyone!
