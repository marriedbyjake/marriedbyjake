Jake didn't just marry us, but made our ceremony the most unique, magical, heartfelt, favourite and memorable part of our day. He doesn't just read from a typical celebrant manual or script - he crafts a ceremony that truly represents the couple, their story, and their future into marriage, which is just another way that illustrates how he truly goes above and beyond being a celebrant.

He truly is exceptional and his love for what he does shines through with everything he does. We just knew this is what we wanted not just for us but our guests to experience too. He truly cares about you, your wedding and your marriage, and that was not only felt by us but each of our guests. We feel truly blessed to have met Jake and to have him as such an important and huge part of the most special day of our lives. There are no words powerful enough to express how grateful we are to Jake for everything and this journey he has gone through with us. We would get married over and over again just to have Jake be there to make it perfect all over again.
