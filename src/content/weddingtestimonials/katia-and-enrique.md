Jake's professionalism and knowledge put us at complete ease and made us feel that we could trust him from very early on. He communicated well throughout the whole process - assisting us with the legalities, explaining the structure of a wedding ceremony and answering any questions we had.

Jake was very engaging during our ceremony which made the guests feel welcome and entertained. He also personalised the ceremony to who we are as a couple by working alongside us prior to the day. We had guests complimenting us on how fantastic he was all evening.

We couldn't have asked for a better celebrant!

Lots of love,
