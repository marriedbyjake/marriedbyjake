Jake from the beginning was so helpful and full of ideas and information. We instantly felt comfortable around him, so it made sense to have him not only as our celebrant but also as our MC. He really understood the type of wedding that we wanted and helped portray that with our guests!

He was excellent at guiding us through the ceremony, which really eased our nerves. We had so many of our wedding guests tell us how much they loved him and that they wanted him for their weddings!
