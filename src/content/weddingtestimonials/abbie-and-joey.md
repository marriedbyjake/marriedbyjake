What a weekend it was! We spent 9 months planning a stunning wedding with all our closest friends and family out in the Scenic Rim and were so excited to have Jake as both our celebrant and MC.

Everything leading up to our day seemed to be going wrong due to COVID, supply issues, and distribution stock levels at all-time lows. We got a little rain throughout the week, but that's good luck! The day of our wedding came and it was full steam ahead.

Sitting in the makeup chair, I got the call that our venue was flooded in and our day was cancelled. Never fear! Jake was here! Our bridal party swung into action calling all our vendors to relocate to where I, the bride, stayed the night prior. Jake arrived with energy and enthusiasm, lifting all our spirits.

Our ceremony went off without a hitch - we even noticed the finest detail of our wedding certificate location being updated! He thought of everything whilst we were in a state of shock and the day went by in a blur. Words cannot describe how grateful we are for everything he did to make our Plan B (that was planned in mere hours) run even better than our original plans.

After our ceremony, Jake helped ferry our guests through the rain to the local pub and ensured everyone was seated with a hot meal and drink in hand. What could've been a disaster turned out to be a dream wedding that we'll never forget.

After such an eventful day we were shattered and needed to call the night off earlier than planned. I still have the image in my mind of Jake soaked from the rain as he ran through the streets looking for our coach service to take our guests back to their accommodation.

Jake, you went above and beyond, taking the weight off our shoulders and we would highly recommend him to anyone planning their big day. If we had our day over again, we wouldn't change a thing!
