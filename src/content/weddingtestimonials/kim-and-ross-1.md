We had our wedding in June 2024 and found Jake to be the most amazing wedding celebrant and MC. After filling in quite a lot of information for Jake, it makes sense as he really takes the time to read your answers and requests. Jake gives you advice and tailors your ceremony to suit what you desire as a couple.

With this information, it gives Jake a very good understanding of who you are as a couple, how you would like your ceremony to be, and more about you as individuals too - a well worth exercise. Jake kept our ceremony light-hearted, funny yet meaningful, and he had everyone's attention from start to finish. He listened to what we wanted and he delivered.

Jake is calm and collected and he reminded us that it is not a performance and he will be there to guide us through our special day. We were so impressed by Jake. When it came to the MC part, Jake was funny but not silly and had everyone's attention. He does not make himself the centre of attention and keeps his introductions and speeches short. He is funny, quick-witted and is an absolute delight to be around.

Jake was also kind enough to blend in with our colour scheme and he wore a suit which matched perfectly. Jake is so easy to talk to and very approachable. We can't thank Jake enough and would highly recommend him.
