Daniel and I are so grateful that we chose Jake to be our celebrant for our wedding in June. The ceremony was the most daunting part of the planning process for us. As two introverts, we were very nervous to have all eyes on us and to put our very low-key, private relationship on display for our family and friends. So much so we considered a registry ceremony before chatting with Jake. Very glad we didn't go down that road!

Our first chat with Jake helped allay all of our fears and gave us confidence that we could tailor the ceremony to our style - low-key, short and sweet! Jake went above and beyond on the day. The words of encouragement pre-ceremony meant the world to us and made us as relaxed as you can be before walking down the aisle.

We have no words for just how great the ceremony was. Many of our guests complimented us on our choice of celebrant - amazing, hilarious, warm and engaging were just a few words guests used to describe Jake!

Finally, we would also like to commend Jake's patience and kindness during our postponement in 2020. He was amazing to deal with during a chaotic time and having him marry us was well and truly worth the wait for us!
