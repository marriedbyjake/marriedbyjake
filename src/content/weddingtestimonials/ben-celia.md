Words cannot express how grateful we are to Jake for the most perfect wedding ceremony! While planning our wedding, I realised with hindsight that the ceremony is much more important than the reception. Though I focussed about 90% on our reception and only 10% on our ceremony initially, when my father walked me into that chapel, I was overwhelmed by love.

There were beaming smiles everywhere wishing us well, and seeing the look on Ben's face will stay with me for a lifetime. Jake was instrumental in making this moment perfect - he calmed Ben before I walked in and said a prayer for him before the wedding started. He also welcomed and calmed me when I arrived, helping my anxiety leave so I could focus on the love in the room.

The ceremony was absolutely perfect, and I can't tell you how many people have complimented what Jake said, how it reminded them of their own vows, and how great his delivery was, especially how clear his voice was in that large room. Everyone was incredibly impressed, and my Dad even said Jake was 'an absolute find.'

Just as Jake told me to, I held onto Ben's hand and didn't get separated - just like us from now on. Thank you again, Jake, it was a truly beautiful moment that we will remember forever.
