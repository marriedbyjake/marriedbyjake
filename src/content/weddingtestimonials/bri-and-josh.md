I honestly cannot begin to express how amazing Jake was throughout our entire wedding process. In terms of planning our day, Jake made every step so smooth and simple, and for two people who really struggle with making final decisions, this was invaluable to us!

From helping us organise the ceremony to giving us advice on how to write our vows, to providing suggestions on how to improve the run sheet - Jake was there every step of the way and made everything so easy.

On the actual day, we received non-stop compliments on how amazing Jake was. Our guests could not stop raving about how effortlessly funny he was, and how he really brought the energy to our ceremony and reception.

Jake honestly went above and beyond for us, checking in constantly to see how we were feeling and to make sure we were enjoying ourselves, and also offering to run to the 7/11 to get more batteries for our camera! You could tell he was there to ensure everything was about my husband and I and that we were at the centre of every decision he made, and we can't thank him enough for that.

We will recommend Jake as a celebrant/MC to anyone who will listen. Thanks so much for everything!
