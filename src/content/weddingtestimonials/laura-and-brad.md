We are so happy that we chose Jake to be a part of our special day and marry us. We had met several other celebrants and didn't click with any of them, but then we spoke with Jake and knew instantly that he was the perfect celebrant for us.

He made all preparations so straightforward and easy, sending us everything we needed to work out how we wanted our ceremony to run and what vows and readings we wanted. He also asked us for information about us and our relationship in order to build it all around us, and completed all relevant legal paperwork which was one less thing to think about! All communication was fast, efficient and fun.

On the day itself, I loved that Jake came to see me whilst I was finishing getting ready. It was so lovely to have a chat and he was able to meet my parents and bridal party before everything started.

The ceremony was absolutely fantastic and better than we could have ever imagined - we enjoyed every moment. Jake had taken all of our provided information and turned it into the most heartfelt and personal ceremony that made us feel so special. All of our guests have commented on how funny and well delivered everything was, which makes it even more special for us.

Jake, we are not able to thank you enough for providing a perfect service to us and the best start to married life.
