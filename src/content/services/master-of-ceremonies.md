## What a good MC actually does

A skilled MC will keep your reception running smoothly without ever stealing the spotlight. They:

- Arrive fully prepared and familiar with your run sheet  
- Make thoughtful adjustments before and during the event  
- Minimise interruptions so you can enjoy your evening  
- Help guests feel comfortable and aware of what’s happening  
- Ensure smooth transitions to maintain momentum  
- Use the microphone sparingly and with purpose  
- Welcome guests and check in throughout the night  
- Support speech-makers and settle any nerves  
- Work collaboratively with photographers, videographers, caterers and entertainers  
- Bring warmth, confidence and humour — without awkwardness  
- Stay focused on the job rather than socialising
