A great wedding celebrant does more than legal paperwork and a few words at the front. Jake Smith brings calm, clarity and genuine warmth to every ceremony he leads.

Since 2007, he’s helped hundreds of couples feel confident, supported and completely themselves as they take one of life’s biggest steps. His role is to guide you, steady the room and create a ceremony that feels personal, grounded and unmistakably about you.

## What Jake Does as Your Celebrant

### Guides you through the legal process

Jake handles all the official requirements under the Marriage Act 1961. He helps you complete your Notice of Intended Marriage, checks your identification, explains the legal steps in plain language and lodges everything after the ceremony so your marriage is properly registered.

### Helps plan a meaningful ceremony

Every couple is different, and Jake takes the time to understand what matters to you. He walks you through the structure, tone and flow of the ceremony, helps shape your story, and supports you with vow writing if you need it. Nothing is generic, nothing is templated — the ceremony should feel like you.

### Brings calm confidence on the day

Jake arrives early, checks in with key people and works closely with photographers, videographers and your venue to make sure everything runs smoothly. His calm, steady presence helps settle nerves and keep the moment feeling warm and relaxed.

### Leads a ceremony with clarity and care

A good celebrant knows how to hold a room without taking it over. Jake speaks with warmth, keeps the focus on you, and guides your guests through an experience that feels joyful, genuine and well paced. Guests often walk away saying they felt part of something real.

### Welcomes couples of any belief

Although Jake is a Christian pastor, most of the weddings he performs are not religious. He’s comfortable shaping ceremonies that reflect your background, personality and comfort level — with or without faith elements. His approach is inclusive, kind and never pushy.

## What You Can Expect

- Clear communication from your first enquiry
- Supportive planning conversations
- Guidance on vows, structure and logistics
- A ceremony that feels personal and engaging
- Someone who works seamlessly with all your vendors
- A celebrant who genuinely cares about your marriage, not just your wedding day

Jake’s goal is simple: help create a wedding ceremony that feels meaningful, relaxed and well supported — and marks the beginning of a strong, joyful marriage.
