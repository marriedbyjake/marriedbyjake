Jake leads Christian wedding ceremonies with a gentle, steady approach that helps couples feel present and supported. As an ordained pastor he works closely with you to create a service that reflects your convictions, honours your families, and keeps guests engaged without feeling lectured.

What a Christian ceremony can include:

*   Opening welcome and a clear, warm tone for guests
    
*   Prayer and scripture reading chosen with you
    
*   Short reflection on love, commitment and grace
    
*   Traditional vows or personalised declarations
    
*   Exchange of rings, blessing and pronouncement
    

How Jake supports you:

*   Guides you through legal requirements and lodges paperwork
    
*   Helps select readings, prayers and music that fit your style
    
*   Writes and refines the ceremony with you, keeping it personal and succinct
    
*   Coordinates on-the-day flow with your venue and photographer
    

Inclusive for everyone:

Jake welcomes couples and guests from all backgrounds. Ceremonies are kind and accessible—grounded in Christian hope, yet attentive to the room so everyone feels included and respected.
