Presentation is a huge factor for many couples as they choose a celebrant… People are terrified that their celebrant will rock up in something… unusual!! 

I get it… so I’m here to let you know what I usually wear and why.

In addition, what a celebrant wears has an impact on how a wedding feels. Everyone has their own style but I think we should all exhibit the three 'C's... (that I just made up!)

Care: We should take a good hard look in the mirror before we arrive! It should be obvious from our presentation that your wedding is special and deserving of our best effort.

Current: For celebrants, presentation should be an ‘ongoing’ not a ‘once-off’ expense!

Cleanliness: Honestly, I've seen celebrants with stains on their clothes... in their profile pics...
