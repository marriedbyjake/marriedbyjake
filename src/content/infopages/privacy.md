We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

When you make an enquiry or contact Jake Smith, we collect only the details needed to respond, check availability, and provide celebrant services. Your information is never sold, and it’s only shared with government authorities when required to legally register your marriage under the Marriage Act 1961.

Any data you submit through forms or email is stored securely and used solely for communication, booking, and legal paperwork relating to your wedding. You can request access to, or deletion of, your personal information at any time by contacting Jake directly at [hello@marriedbyjake.com](mailto:hello@marriedbyjake.com).

By using this website, you agree to this policy.
